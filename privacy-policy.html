Privacy Policy for ZomBlitz on iPhone

Last updated: 29 October 2026

ZomBlitz: Gun Heroes ("ZomBlitz", "the game") is developed and published by Roy Greenberg. This policy applies to the iPhone application on the App Store with the bundle identifier com.greenberg.hordefield.

The short version

The game has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no purchases, and does not sell or share personal information. If you agree, it sends game statistics and crash reports to Google's Firebase service so we can find what is hard and fix what breaks; if you do not agree, it sends nothing. Your progress stays on your device.

What the game does not collect

  • No name, phone number or account of any kind (an email address only if you write to us).
  • No precise location. Firebase works out a rough area (country or city) from the connection's IP address; the address itself is masked and not kept.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): the game does not collect it and does not ask to track you. It shows no ads.

Game statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google, for us. Nothing is sent before you answer the statistics question that appears when you first start the game.

  • Your choice: nothing is sent unless you answer "YES". If you answer "NO, THANKS", or switch STATISTICS off in Settings, no statistics and no crash reports are sent.
  • What is sent if you answer "YES": game events such as a stage started or ended and how it went, and coins earned and spent; crash reports (what the game was doing when it stopped, the phone model and system version), with basic facts about how the game runs (for example when it was opened or sent to the background, and the type of network connection); general facts sent with every event (phone model, system version, language, game version, when the game was first opened, and how many times it has been played); an installation identifier that Firebase creates for this copy of the game; and the identifier for vendor (IDFV), a code Apple gives each developer for its own apps on a device; it is not the advertising identifier.
  • Your Player ID: statistics are kept for this installation together with your Player ID, so a deletion request can find them. The same Player ID appears in emails you send us from the game.
  • How long: Analytics data about your installation is kept for 14 months; crash reports for 90 days. Totals and summary reports that do not identify any player or installation may be kept longer.

You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings.

Google processes this data only to provide these services to us, under terms that give it the same or equal protection as this policy.

Writing to us

CONTACT US in Settings opens your own mail app with a message that includes the game's version, your phone's model and system, and your Player ID (a random code made by the game, not tied to your phone or any account). We receive it only if you send it, together with your email address. Support emails are deleted when the issue is closed, or on request.

Reminders

The game first says in its own window what reminders are for; then your iPhone asks for permission to show notifications. If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your phone - for example when your hearts are full, when a daily gift is ready, before a reward season ends, and after you have been away. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us to create them. You can turn all of them, or each kind, off in the game's settings or your phone's settings.

Checking the time

To stop timed rewards from being collected early by changing the phone's clock, the game asks Apple's web server (www.apple.com) for the current time when it starts, and again every two minutes until it gets an answer. The request carries nothing about you or your game. As with any internet request, the server sees your device's IP address, under Apple's own privacy policy. The game does not record or keep it.

Where your progress is stored

Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device, protected against editing. They are not backed up to any server by the game. Deleting the game deletes them. A backup of your iPhone that you make yourself (iCloud or a computer) may include them, under Apple's terms.

The App Store

The App Store handles the download and may collect information about it under Apple's own privacy policy. We receive only the aggregated, anonymous statistics Apple gives every developer (for example, how many people installed the game).

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the App Store's Kids category. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete the statistics kept for your installation: write to the address below with the Player ID shown in the game's Settings. If you have any other question about your privacy, write to us too.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it does with information, this policy is updated before that version is released, and the date above changes.

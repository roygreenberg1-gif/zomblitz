ZomBlitz support

ZomBlitz: Gun Heroes for iPhone · published by Roy Greenberg

Contact: email playrg.games@gmail.com with a short description of the problem, your iPhone model (for example "iPhone 14 Pro") and the game version (shown at the bottom of the game's own Settings). The CONTACT US button in the game's Settings fills these in for you. We answer in English, usually within two working days.

Your progress

  • Your progress is saved on your iPhone only; the game has no account and no online save. Deleting the game deletes it. A backup of your iPhone (iCloud or a computer) may include it, and restoring that backup may bring it back.

Found a bug?

  • Email us what happened, what you were doing just before, and a screenshot if you can.

Your data

  • The game sends game statistics and crash reports only if you agree, as described in the Privacy Policy; it has no account, no ads and no purchases.
  • To have your statistics deleted, write to us with the Player ID shown at the bottom of the game's Settings.
  • The rules for playing are in the Terms of Service.
